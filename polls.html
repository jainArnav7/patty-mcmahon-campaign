The difference between leading questions and neutral questions.

Leading vs. Neutral Questions

A leading question pushes the person answering toward a certain answer. A neutral question does not tell the person what they are supposed to think. Good polling uses neutral questions so the results are more honest.

Leading

Leading examples

"Don't you think the government spends too much taxpayer money?"

"Wouldn't lowering taxes help Colorado families?"

Neutral

Neutral examples

"How would you describe the government's use of taxpayer money?"

"What is your opinion on lowering state taxes in Colorado?"
