Interest Groups & Campaign Finance

Suggested outreach

Suggested PACs and Groups to Reach Out To

These are possible groups for the campaign to contact. We have not found evidence that they gave money or endorsed Patty McMahon.

1. Libertarian Party of Colorado

Why they matter: Patty McMahon is running as a Libertarian. The state party could help more people learn about her campaign, reach Libertarian voters, and possibly find volunteers or other help.

Effect on campaign strategy: If the party supported her, the campaign could spend more time reaching voters who value individual freedom and less government involvement. This could help in a race with candidates from major parties.

Expected policy promises: Support for individual freedom and limits on government power. These are expected areas of agreement, not promises made in exchange for support.

2. American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU)

Why they matter: The ACLU works to protect people's rights and freedoms. This connects with Patty's stated support for privacy, free speech, due process, and equal treatment under the law.

Effect on campaign strategy: If the ACLU supported her, the campaign could reach voters who care about civil liberties and protecting people's rights from government overreach.

Expected policy promises: Support for free speech, privacy, and due process. These are expected areas of agreement, not promises made in exchange for support.

3. Institute for Justice

Why they matter: The Institute for Justice focuses on individual rights, economic freedom, and limits on government power. These ideas connect with Patty's support for individual liberty and constitutional limits on government.

Effect on campaign strategy: If the group supported her, it could help the campaign reach voters who think government has too much power and explain why individual rights matter.

Expected policy promises: Support for constitutional rights and individual freedom. These are expected areas of agreement, not promises made in exchange for support.
