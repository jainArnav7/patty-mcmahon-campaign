Meet Patty McMahon

Libertarian candidate for the U.S. House of Representatives in Colorado's 6th Congressional District.

About Patty McMahon

Patty McMahon is the Libertarian candidate for Colorado's 6th Congressional District. She graduated from Metropolitan State University of Denver in 1989 and went on to spend 35 years with the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC).

She began her EEOC career as an investigator and later worked in outreach and education. For 25 years, she served as a congressional liaison, working with congressional offices in Colorado and across the country. She also led Region VIII work for the White House Initiative on Asian Americans, Native Hawaiians, and Pacific Islanders.

In 2004, McMahon was chosen for an EEOC team that reorganized the agency. She says the changes saved taxpayers millions of dollars without layoffs. She has served on Colorado nonprofit and civic boards and says she has lived in the 6th District for 26 years.

McMahon's campaign emphasizes congressional oversight, constitutional limits on government power, civil liberties, and avoiding unnecessary military intervention. She describes her approach as bringing people together across political differences and putting constituents ahead of party agendas.

What a U.S. Representative Does

A U.S. representative serves in the House of Representatives. Representatives write and vote on laws, decide how federal money is spent, and ask questions when government agencies act. They also speak for people in their district. The House can investigate problems in the government and vote to impeach a federal official. The House's guide to how laws are made explains how bills move through Congress, and the U.S. Constitution explains the House's powers.

McMahon's 35 years at the EEOC and 25 years working with congressional offices gave her experience with federal agencies and Congress. Her campaign says she would focus on oversight, taxpayer money, and making sure Congress does its job. That experience is one reason supporters may see her as a good fit for the office.
