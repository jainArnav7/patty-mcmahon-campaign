Voter Strategy

Winning Independent Voters in CO-6

A plan built around personal freedom, taxes, and government spending.

Target Voters

Focus on independent voters in CO-6. Strategy includes social media, online ads, and community events focusing on personal freedom, taxes, and government spending.

Social Media

Reach independent voters where they already spend time, with messages on personal freedom, taxes, and government spending.

Online Ads

Use low-cost online ads to put the campaign in front of people who are not locked into a major party.

Community Events

Show up in person so voters can hear the message directly and ask questions.
