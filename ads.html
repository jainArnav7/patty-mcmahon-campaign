Ad 1 · Positive

Freedom and Accountability

Focuses on freedom and accountability.

"We the people deserve a government that listens and cares about what people have to say. Patty McMahon is running for Congress as a libertarian. And she believes that we should have freedom and rights protected by the government. She also believes the government should be transparent about their spending so that we know where our taxpayer dollars are going to. She wants people to have a bigger voice and more control over their own lives. Also, people have bigger input on what happens in the government. Because she makes your voice matter. Learn more about Patty McMahon and her ideas for Colorado's 6th Congressional District."

Ad 2 · Attack

Jason Ray Clark's Tax Plan

Criticizes Jason Ray Clark's Tax Plan.

"Jason Ray Clark wants to be the leader of Colorado’s 6th District. But he has a plan that could change how our state gets its money. Clark wants to get rid of Colorado’s 5% state income tax. He says this will save people money. But to do this, he also wants to make big cuts to government spending. This could mean less money for important things that people in Colorado need. Saving money sounds good, but we also need money for our schools, communities, and other important services such as 911. Coloradans should know how these changes could affect their lives before making such a big decision."
