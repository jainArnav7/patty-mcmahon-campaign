Issues

Political ideology, civil rights, and civil liberties.

Political Ideology

Patty McMahon is running for Congress as a Libertarian. Libertarians believe people should be free to make their own decisions as long as they respect other people's rights... Patty wants Congress to keep the president's power in check and make sure the government follows the Constitution. She wants Congress to keep an eye on how the government spends people's tax money. She cares about protecting people's rights, including free speech and privacy, and supports avoiding unnecessary wars.

Civil Rights & Liberties

Civil rights and civil liberties are the rights and freedoms everyone should have. Libertarians think people should be able to make their own decisions.
